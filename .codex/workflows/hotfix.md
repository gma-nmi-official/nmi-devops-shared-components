# Hotfix

Follow the [bugfix path](bugfix.md) with focused documentation and the same
[approval policy](../../AGENTS.md). Record incident impact, owner, target
revision, minimum correction, validation, rollback, and deployment risk.
Urgency does not authorize skipping required tests, review, or approvals.
If checks cannot run, record BLOCKED and present the risk and decision needed;
do not label the release ready. After an authorized release, verify service
health and capture follow-up work and deferred nonblocking improvements.
