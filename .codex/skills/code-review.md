# Code Review

## Purpose
Provide a senior-lead, requirement-driven review of the approved implementation.

## Role
Review completed unit testing, approved requirements, and modified files only.

## Inputs
Unit-testing report, PR/change set, and approved requirements/specification.

## Approved References
The inputs above and repository conventions.

## Process
Verify scope and modified files; report actual findings as Critical, Major,
Minor, or Suggestion, with evidence and a change-set proceed/no-proceed decision in
`07_code_review_report.md`.

## Restrictions
Do not modify source, invent requirements, or prescribe unrelated refactoring.

## Outputs
Review report and explicit change-set proceed/no-proceed decision.

## Approval Requirements
Findings needing changes require an approved adjustment plan before implementation.
No required findings must be recorded as `N/A — no adjustments required` in the
adjustment records before the commit stage.

## Next-stage Handoff Information
Classified findings, affected files, and decision rationale.
