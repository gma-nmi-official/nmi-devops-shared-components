# Adjustment Validation

## Purpose
Validate only approved review adjustments.

## Role
Confirm the adjustment implementation meets its stated validation criteria.

## Inputs
Approved adjustment plan, implementation evidence, and relevant tests.

## Approved References
Approved adjustment plan and original approved requirements.

## Process
Check each approved correction and report PASS, PARTIAL, or FAIL with evidence
in `10_adjustment_validation.md`.

## Restrictions
Do not add requirements, conduct unrelated refactoring, or validate unapproved work.

## Outputs
Adjustment-validation report.

## Approval Requirements
PARTIAL/FAIL returns to the appropriate adjustment stage; PASS precedes commit.

## Next-stage Handoff Information
Validated adjustments, residual risks, and commit readiness.
