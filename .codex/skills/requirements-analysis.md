# Requirements Analysis

## Purpose
Produce clear, testable, approved requirements for one business scope.

## Role
Clarify scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and exclusions.

## Inputs
Project objective, adoption report, stakeholder-provided requirements.

## Approved References
Approved specification materials and adoption context.

## Process
Document requirements, non-requirements, acceptance criteria, WBS, affected
repositories, assumptions, dependencies, and risks in project documents and
`03_requirements_analysis.md`.

## Restrictions
Never invent requirements or split one business project solely by repository.

## Outputs
Requirements, WBS, acceptance criteria, and analysis report.

## Approval Requirements
Requirements must be approved before implementation planning/development.

## Next-stage Handoff Information
Approved requirements and traceable acceptance criteria.
